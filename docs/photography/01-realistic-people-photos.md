# Glorysake — Fresh candid photography direction, version 3

## Current brief
Start fresh. Prioritise ordinary believable moments, accurate reference likeness and natural behaviour over decorative polish. Generate five different desktop scenes and five corresponding mobile compositions. All three uploaded portraits are appearance references. They do not establish names, testimonials, employment, membership, earnings or any other personal facts.

## Master prompt — append the relevant shot
Make an ordinary, believable candid photograph, not an advertising portrait. Preserve the exact person's facial structure, skin tone, hairline, hairstyle and natural proportions from the supplied reference. Reference controls likeness only: change pose and gaze to suit the scene. Existing daylight only, neutral white balance, natural contrast, moderately deep focus with recognisable background, plain real-world colours. Skin has subtle unevenness and normal shine, hair and clothes have ordinary fine detail; no beauty retouching. Observational eye-level camera, 35mm lens at f/5.6, relaxed unplanned framing. No cinematic grade, golden glow, volumetric light, neon, faux film damage, artificially blurred foreground, glamour styling or exaggerated smiles. No text, logos, visible screen content or earnings claims. One photo, no panels.

## Mobile generation — separate compositions
Use the selected desktop image AND its original portrait reference(s). Generate a separate 9:16 vertical composition; do not crop a face out of a landscape image or stretch it.

Preserve each person's own face, hairstyle, outfit, skin texture, natural lighting and quiet behaviour. Keep important details within the central 84% width. No new people, props, text or beauty treatment. Do not bake a black gradient into the image. Compose the upper third as plausible quiet architecture or environment. Avoid floating walls, unsupported beams and impossible windows. Maintain the real spatial relationship of the background.

Export desktop at 1920×1080 and mobile at 1080×1920. WebP quality 80 is the first choice. Prioritise visible skin and hair detail over a hard byte limit. Try to keep each desktop under 250 KB and each mobile under 150 KB; record exceptions rather than hiding aggressive quality loss. Keep original generated masters separately. Do not claim an upscale creates additional captured detail.

## Visual verification
Judge actual generated outputs, not the length of the prompt. Compare faces with the ORIGINAL references and compare each mobile image with its desktop scene. Check facial structure, believable expression, skin texture, hair, finger count where visible, hand-object contact, pen grip, table/window geometry, outfit continuity, ordinary light and background consistency. Regenerate only an actual visible defect with one targeted change. A fresh mobile composition may differ in small environmental details; do not claim pixel identity.

Review at full image and face/hand detail scales. Record what passed and any uncertainties. No numeric “AI-ness” score, no claims of “10,000× less AI”, and no claim that synthetic scenes are real documentary evidence. Visual review cannot guarantee that an image will be perceived as natural by every viewer.

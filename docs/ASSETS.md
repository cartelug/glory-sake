# Asset provenance

## Owner-provided assets

`logo-white.png`, `logo-dark.png`, `assets/fonts/` (Brighter Sans Bold; Sky Text Light, Regular, Bold; CC BY 4.0, credit in the site footer) were extracted from the user-provided GLORYSAKE Ultimate Website Masterplan 2026 PDF. Fonts are the embedded subsets supplied in that document; obtain full licensed webfont files before extending the character set or localizing into additional languages.

## Custom generated artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool on 10 November 2026. Optimized derivatives are project assets, not factual photographs of members.

### Hero (`hero.webp`, `hero-mobile.webp`)

Premium ultrawide website hero background for a modern African digital tasks and rewards platform. Cinematic editorial 3D sculpture: an elegant folded ribbon looping upward into an abstract four-point star, pearlescent lavender material and brushed champagne-gold inner edge, floating glass spheres and matte midnight violet architectural steps. Right-weighted composition with empty dark left half for the headline. No text, logo, UI, coins or money. Mobile derivative crops the same artwork.

### Community (`community.webp`)

Editorial illustration in a photographic style: three adult East African friends at a sunlit contemporary Kampala cafe terrace, candid laughter around a smartphone and laptop, cream/lavender/muted green clothing, tropical plants and natural afternoon light. No text, logos, money or visible app UI. Labeled illustrative in the website.

### Social preview / favicon

Social preview composes the hero with live brand typography. Favicon uses the supplied white mark on midnight. These are deterministic derivatives rather than newly generated logos.

## MTN and Airtel logos

`assets/mtn-logo.svg` and `assets/airtel-logo.svg` were taken from each network's own Uganda website (mtn.co.ug and airtel.co.ug) on 11 November 2026. They are the parent-brand logos, not the separate MTN MoMo or Airtel Money product marks. They are trademarks of their owners, shown only to identify the payment network and not as an endorsement. Before live payments launch, request the approved MoMo and Airtel Money logo files and brand guidelines from each operator and replace these files if required.
